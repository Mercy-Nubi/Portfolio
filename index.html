02 · About meMercy Nubi · Portfolio 2026
Open to work

Hello! I’m Mercy Nubi.

I design products, and then I can’t resist building them too. I start by asking who it’s for and what’s getting in their way, sketch the simplest flow that fixes it, and polish it in Figma until it feels obvious. Then I vibecode it into a real, working frontend with AI tools, because a design you can click beats one you have to imagine. I’m also a motion designer: I animate interfaces and brand stories in After Effects, so the product doesn’t just look right, it feels alive. Three years in, most of my work lives in fintech and Web3, where a calm, clear screen is what makes people trust you with their money.

How I workMercy Nubi · Portfolio 2026

From problem
to shipped screen.

  1. Understand

    Talk to users and the team, map the flow, and agree on what success looks like.

  2. Design

    Wireframes, then high-fidelity screens and a component system in Figma.

  3. Build

    Vibecode the frontend with AI tools so the design runs in a real browser.

  4. Refine

    Test the live build, fix the rough edges, and hand off clean, working code.
