About

I design the product, then I build it.

I'm a product designer with 3 years of experience who also works on the frontend. I start with the problem: who the product is for, what's getting in their way, and the simplest flow that fixes it. Then I turn that into clear interfaces and design systems.

I don't stop at the Figma file. I vibecode my designs into real, responsive frontends with AI-assisted tools. That lets me test ideas in the browser, hand engineers something that already works, and keep the shipped product true to the design.

Most of my work is in fintech and Web3: payments, wallets and commerce, where a clear interface is what earns people's trust.

How I work

From problem to shipped screen

  1. Understand

    Talk to users and the team, map the flow, and agree on what success looks like.

  2. Design

    Wireframes, then high-fidelity screens and a component system in Figma.

  3. Build

    Vibecode the frontend with AI tools so the design runs in a real browser.

  4. Refine

    Test the live build, fix the rough edges, and hand off clean, working code.
