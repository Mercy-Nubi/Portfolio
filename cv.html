Profile

Hi, I'm Mercy. I design products, and then I can't resist building them too. I start by asking who it's for and what's getting in their way, sketch the simplest flow that fixes it, and polish it in Figma until it feels obvious. Then I vibecode it into a real, working frontend with AI tools, because a design you can click beats one you have to imagine. Three years in, most of my work lives in fintech and Web3, where a calm, clear screen is what makes people trust you with their money.
