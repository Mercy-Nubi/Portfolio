BRAND IDENTITY & PACKAGING · 2026

Pasta & Pop for the fun side of lunch.

fizzili pairs twirly cup pasta with fizzy sodas. One brand, two cravings, one bold meal deal for a younger, hungrier crowd.

01 · Idea

Twirl it. Sip it. Repeat.

Gen Z eats on the move. fizzili turns the quick lunch into the best ten minutes of the day.

Brand
fizzili, Pasta & Pop
Market
Gen Z and young millennials
Products
4 cup pastas, 4 sodas, 1 meal deal
Designer
Mercy Nubi

The name says it all: fizzy plus fusilli. The identity is built on two shapes that belong to the product, the twist of the pasta and the round of the bubble, and every part of the system grows from them.

Cans spin in twisted-ribbon stripes like a fusilli spiral. Flavour labels sit in wavy, bubbly frames. Glossy 3D pasta and soda bubbles float through every shot, so the two products always feel like one world.

Every pasta comes with a soda made for it: Tomato Penne with Cherry Fizz, Cheesy Fusilli with Citrus Burst. Comfort food, made playful again.
